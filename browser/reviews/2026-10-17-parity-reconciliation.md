## What the fixtures establish

Source script fixtures establish particular command/state branches, imported data, capacity/retry behavior and persistence with explicit host ports. Actual-binding fixtures extract committed production functions or construct the actual World/VM with injected rendering/UI/clock/battle ports. They provide stronger integration evidence than copied algorithms, but remain offline fixtures. A supplied battle win does not prove that the battle can be naturally won; a constructed start state does not prove that earlier campaign traversal reaches it.

Historical documents keep their original source hashes, pending blockers and test counts. Later implemented features are reconciled here rather than rewriting earlier snapshots to claim approval of code they never inspected. The audio bundle distinguishes the first external-renderer read denial from the successful follow-up; the host review's later-component qualifications are likewise narrower than a whole-current-main approval.

No requirement is promoted to Verified by this change. P03-001 stays Pending; P03-002/P03-003 are Implemented with the limits above. The 295 requirements, 540 source maps and 832 script/movement names are inventories, not completed gameplay. This change does not bulk-promote any command or map status.
