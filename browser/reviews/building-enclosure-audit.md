# Exterior enclosure audit

This is geometric and alpha-aware evidence, not approval of the building artwork. A mirrored front door can be fully opaque and still make an unacceptable rear elevation. The separate visual inventory/gallery tracks that review.

Run from the repository root:

```sh
node --experimental-transform-types browser/scripts/audit_building_enclosures.ts --self-test
node --experimental-transform-types browser/scripts/audit_building_enclosures.ts --candidate=all --strict
```

Add `--dense` for 321 directions per core: 64 azimuths at each nonvertical pitch plus zenith. The default uses 81 directions for faster triage. Both modes record their exact pitch/azimuth pattern, preserve strict misses and remain finite samples.

`--candidate=all` invokes the game's `closeBuildingExterior` dispatcher on a fresh placed clone. Original, shared-repair and legacy entrance-repair stages remain in the report for comparison. `--assets=1-40,1-39` restricts the building families while still including their actual nearby door attachments. `--output=/tmp/enclosures.json` preserves the checked-in report. Named partial candidate helpers are supported for development only.

Partial helper names, modules and execution order are discovered from the actual dispatcher imports/calls, including newly integrated families. Ordinary-house classification imports `HOUSE_BUILDING_MODELS` from the real builder rather than duplicating its IDs. Explicit gate/open-air categories remain separate source classifications. Additional manual source classifications come from the hashed `building-category-triage.json` registry and must match each exact original GLB SHA. This records source identity and category evidence, not visual approval of repaired geometry.

The inventory includes every major visible bank-one structure in route/city/town matrices, the exterior Ilex/Viridian Forest gates, and thin League gates. All smaller candidates retain their source key, bounds, transform and exclusion reason. Canonical model IDs, texture contexts and placements have separate counts.

The audit reads used indices, material groups, skinned source vertices and complete placement transforms. Shadow/foliage cards do not define the building body. Visible shadow-named door cards still participate in the alpha-aware ray test. Separately placed native `DOOR` models are included in their imported bind pose; omitting those incorrectly reports front entrance recesses as missing walls. Verified co-located body/roof assemblies, currently Dome 179 plus Dome2 226, retain separate constituent rows and explicitly list their secondary blockers.

Each body is sampled on a 3×3 grid at three wall-relative heights. Seeds need roof coverage and horizontal support near both the floor and the sampled height. This rejects air underneath an overhanging fascia and records every rejection. A model with no confident core remains a manual-review item.

Bell Tower 9 and Sprout Tower 54 have pedestals below their enclosed walls, so the global-floor hull cannot establish an interior. Their explicit alternatives use nine and three measured native timber story ranges, a 3×3 grid inside the actual upper wall footprint, and three heights per story. Every point must still have a native roof above and at least three wall blockers. The report records the source ranges and seed strategy. Raised shrine 71 similarly samples only its measured chamber above the platform rails; the open space between its stilts is preserved.

Frontier Tower 262 similarly has a projecting entrance landing below its body and rear fins outside its real wall rectangle. Its explicit 27-point body grid requires the four exact native side-wall bands and three real horizontal blockers. The native model's roof absence is retained as an original-stage failure and recorded in `sourceRoofAbsences`, rather than excluding every body point before testing the new roof.

Factory 265 and Arcade 266 retain measured body grids below their native roof/deck elevations and behind their western approaches. Their original models omit two large side elevations, so requiring three original blockers would incorrectly remove every interior point. Exact native body vertices guard these profiles, and original horizontal support deficits are recorded instead of censored. Factory roof absences within its proven lower body remain original-stage failures, like Tower 262; Arcade points require actual source deck coverage. S.S. Aqua 168 samples its measured enclosed cabin in both actual port placements, requiring its real source ceiling; this is explicitly cabin coverage, with no room-enclosure claim for its open decks or hull.

Underground stair headhouse 290 has an opaque sloping main roof below a raised glass ornament. Any grid seed above that exact source roof plane is outside the body. Rejections retain the full seed position, local position and an actual opaque down-hit on the source main roof; the decorative glass does not turn that pocket into an interior.

Source-equivalent stairhead 339 uses the same guarded roof classification. House 236 has a veranda in front of its opaque room wall: rejected veranda seeds must lie over the actual clipped source deck and have a real opaque source facade point behind them. These witnesses are recorded independently of any generated repair.

Gates 35/36/246/247 use source-measured central room grids behind the native door cards. The generic hull's plinth/awning/entry-alcove grid is retained as a replaced diagnostic, together with actual opaque card/floor witnesses. Northern approach modules are explicitly event-driven additions, not source-authored northern cards. Neither those exterior entries nor the space under a rear canopy constitutes a closed-room core.

Port 218 and prize exchange 240 reject bounded exterior front recess seeds only when their original jamb coordinates match and both the opaque backplate and the source floor are hit at the actual seed projection. These original exterior pockets are not interior room leaks, and their rejection makes no functional-warp claim. Name Rater87 similarly uses its actual nonplanar opaque backplate, exact native floor triangles and six-vertex inclined curtain opening; each excluded seed records the card, floor and aperture hit points. A broad curtain bounding box is insufficient.

Pal Park gate 295 similarly rejects only its bounded front alcove over the native Y12 floor and ahead of its opaque Z26 backplate, with actual source-hit witnesses. Context73's north passage is a separate event-derived repair: its aperture is allowed only in the candidate stage after confirming the applied family, opaque generated header/sides, and the exact pair of `passageOnly` hints. Its repaired bounds and provenance are recorded, and `repairedPassageAllowances` never asserts a source-authored opening or confirmed functional warp.

Lighthouse, radio mast/observation elevations and silo cores use supplementary measured native grids inside their actual shafts. Lighthouse projected searchlight triangles are identified by their native `hikari` UV strip and omitted from room blockers, while the distinct native luminous glazing UV strip remains. The omitted triangle count is recorded.

Each accepted seed casts 81 rays by default, or 321 with `--dense`, across horizontal directions and upward pitches of 8°, 24°, 48°, 72° and 90°. Downward rays are excluded because exterior source models intentionally omit floors. Embedded RGBA PNGs are decoded without image stripping from the actual hit decision. UV interpolation, native repeat/clamp wrapping, `flipY`, OPAQUE versus MASK and blend opacity are respected. The blend threshold is explicitly 0.5.

The report separates absent triangles, alpha-only misses, rejected transparent intersections, authored `h_in` recesses tied to actual front-facing entrances, measured native doorway apertures, event-corridor allowances, invalid seeds and numerical edge ambiguities. Recess exemptions use the native rectangle and its actual hit height, rather than a blanket front-facing exception. Candidate-only unexpected rays identify regions previously blocked by legacy caps.

Facade detection prefers an actual gap at the native doorway over a projecting solid window or planter card. A height allowance can extend beyond the generic event height only when the continuous source gap terminates at a real source header; a gap running out through the facade top does not prove a tall doorway. Original jamb profiles are guarded separately where a recessed header does not share the facade plane.

Pokeathlon Gate 181 has two physically authored north canopies whose full outer widths, lintel height and exit plane are measured from the source. A guarded exception records those exact physical apertures separately from functional warp allowances: their existence does not assert that both exits work in every map. Nearby walls, heights above the lintel and opposite directions remain subject to the same ray checks.

Pokeathlon tent 264 has a tall source porch over its active east approach. Its separate porch allowance requires the actual horizontal roof vertices, uses the inner porch plane and literal roof underside/side bounds, and requires a matching native east warp. Rays above that roof or outside its sides remain failures. Projecting the corridor to an arbitrary outer bounding box would incorrectly classify legal exits from the porch sides.

Factory 265's large western entrance has nonplanar sloping jambs. Its aperture probe is bounded by six literal source jamb/header vertices and a matching native west warp. Four virtual probe triangles describe that opening only; they are not generated building surfaces or blockers. This preserves rays which clear the actual sloping jamb after crossing an inner reference plane, while rejecting the adjacent walls and space above the source header. The exact probe coordinates are included in `sourcePhysicalApertures` and counted separately as native profiled door exits.

Ruins shrine chambers 158/159/161/162 have source-authored physical front entrances without an exported functional warp. Their allowance requires the literal original jambs at X−11.578125/+11.86328125, the Y36 lintel and lower vertical jamb feet reaching Y2, and only rays crossing the bounded Z40 aperture below Y36. No horizontal source floor is asserted for these shrines. These are counted separately as `sourceShrineApertureAllowances`; rear, side and roof rays retain the normal strict checks. Wind turbine 27 and fountains 267/268 are documented open-air props. Burned Tower 8 retains its authored upper fire damage while the ground-story cores remain tested.

A read-only triangle AABB BVH skips triangles outside the ray segment. Bounds have a one-millionth-tile broadphase padding, which can admit extra candidates but never changes the narrowphase. Candidate triangles are restored to their original order before the unchanged Three intersection, near distance, UV and alpha code runs; all hit/rejection counters are retained. `--brute-force` uses the original complete triangle scan. `--verify-broadphase` runs both for each cast and asserts identical exact distances and every hit/alpha/tolerance counter; the report records the verified cast count. Stage geometry is an immutable snapshot for this cache. The [broadphase equivalence receipt](building-broadphase-equivalence.json) records 1,355,607 per-cast comparisons, byte-equal saved geometry bundles, preserved alpha misses/numerical edges/source portals, and a small informal timing comparison.

Strict mode performs exact intersections with no edge tolerance. A separate diagnostic checks whether an exact miss passes within one millionth of a tile of an opaque triangle, recording its material, hit plane, barycentric coordinates and outside-edge distance. These witnesses do not change strict blocked or unexpected counts and require separate adjudication. The self-hit threshold is one ten-millionth of a tile: genuine roof hits only 0.002 tiles from the seed remain blockers, with near-origin intersections counted separately. The optional default edge tolerance is one millionth of a tile and retains its exact-miss rays separately; it is not evidence of a strict seal.

Surface intersections are two-sided for this physical enclosure check. The separate visual gallery must also establish that the outward surface renders with the correct material side and artwork.

Named gates, passage buildings, open-air decorations and unclassified structures retain their raw rays and require source/visual adjudication. They are excluded from the count of confirmed closed-building warnings rather than silently marked passed. Source file and asset SHA-256 hashes identify the geometry/repair snapshot that produced the report; rerun after changing any helper.

The script also rechecks its source/dispatcher/helper hashes and repository revision at completion. `codeSnapshotStable` must be true for a frozen audit; `changedSourceFilesDuringAudit` identifies edits made during a development run. This does not replace freezing the assets and source data for final review.

Every inventoried bank-one placement, including smaller candidates outside the major-structure selection, gets one measured synchronous builder call when a candidate stage is requested. Per-placement records and `builderPerformance` provide elapsed wall-clock milliseconds, family aggregates, p50/p95/max, and slowest placements. Exposed `roofSamples` counters are included; other builders explicitly report unsupported counters. Timings exclude loading, cloning, shared asset repairs, terrain/footings and ray tests. They are Node measurements under current CPU contention, not browser frame-time or whole-map rebuild benchmarks.

The audit does not include runtime terrain deformation or foundation skirts. Those need the field tests and the actual camera gallery as well as this source enclosure check. It also cannot establish full visual approval, complete mesh topology or every possible interior point from a finite ray sample.
